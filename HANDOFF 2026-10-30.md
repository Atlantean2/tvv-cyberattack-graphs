## 1. `CISA KEV exploited vulnerabilities by vendor with ransomware use.png` → **Q1 Exploited Flaws**
- **Title:** Microsoft owns 390 of 1,766 actively exploited flaws — 117 used in ransomware
- **Caption:** CISA KEV entries by vendor (top 12), with known ransomware use marked; catalog v2026.10.30. Source: CISA KEV feed.
- **Label:** DATA
- **Bullets**
  - Oct 30 2026: the catalog has 1,766 confirmed-exploited CVEs, and 361 of them are flagged as used in ransomware campaigns.
  - Microsoft has 390 entries (117 ransomware), Cisco 102, Apple 95, Adobe 83 and Google 76. Edge-device vendors (Fortinet, Ivanti, Citrix) have high ransomware shares.
  - In 2026 so far, CISA has added 282 CVEs, 40 of them Microsoft. Unlike C1, this chart is a vendor view, not a monthly count.
- **Sources**
  - https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog
  - https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/feeds/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json
- **Doubt notes**
  - "Microsoft just has the biggest install base." That's true, and raw counts reflect footprint. The ransomware flag is the money-relevant part.
  - "Unknown" means not confirmed, not "no ransomware use".

## 2. `FBI IC3 reported internet crime losses 2020-2025.png` → **NEW Q3 Ransomware & Costs**
- **Title:** Americans reported a record $20.9B lost to internet crime in 2025, up 26%
- **Caption:** Reported IC3 losses rose from $4.2B (2020) to $20.9B (2025) on 1.0M complaints. Source: FBI IC3 2025 report.
- **Label:** DATA (self-reported)
- **Bullets**
  - 2025: $20.877B lost on 1,008,597 complaints, up 26% from $16.6B in 2024.
  - Cyber-enabled fraud made up $17.7B (85%) of losses; crypto-related losses were $11.4B and investment scams $7.2B.
  - Ransomware reported to IC3 was only 3,600+ complaints and $32M+, because victims mostly don't report business-loss costs.
- **Sources**
  - https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
  - https://www.ic3.gov/
- **Doubt notes**
  - "Some of the rise is just more reporting." Partly, yes: the counts are self-reported and undercount. The FBI says the ransomware figure is "artificially low".
  - Only 2020–2025 are plotted, because earlier years in the report's chart are images without clean labels.

## 3. `IBM global average cost of a data breach 2019-2026.png` → **NEW Q3 Ransomware & Costs**
- **Title:** Average data breach now costs a record $4.99M globally — $11.5M in the US
- **Caption:** Global average breach cost a record $4.99M in 2026 (+12%); US $11.5M. Source: IBM Cost of a Data Breach 2026.
- **Label:** DATA (survey)
- **Bullets**
  - The 2026 report covers 602 organizations breached between Mar 2025 and Feb 2026. The global average was $4.99M, up 12% after a dip to $4.44M in 2025.
  - The US average was $11.50M (2025: $10.22M), a record and more than double the global figure.
  - 39% of breached organizations were hit by ransomware, up from 24% in 2023.
- **Sources**
  - https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach
  - https://assets.ibm.com/adobe/assets/urn%3Aaaid%3Aaem%3A21111142-1251-4369-86fb-57b82f5bb108/original/as/Cost%20of%20a%20Data%20Breach%20Report%202026.pdf
- **Doubt notes**
  - The survey is vendor-sponsored and uses activity-based cost modelling, not audited losses. It's best used for the trend.

## 4. `Ransomware leak site victims per month 2022-2026.png` → **NEW Q3 Ransomware & Costs**
- **Title:** Ransomware leak-site victims: 8,421 so far in 2026, already past 2025's 8,077
- **Caption:** Victims posted on ransomware leak sites per month, through Oct 30 2026. Tracker data from ransomware.live, not confirmed incidents.
- **Label:** TRACKER DATA
- **Bullets**
  - Yearly totals: 3,168 (2022), 5,411 (2023), 6,057 (2024), 8,077 (2025) and 8,421 in 2026 through Oct 30.
  - Aug 2026 was the record month with 1,056 victims, led by Qilin (159) and The Gentlemen (116).
  - Feb 2025 shows a spike of 1,057, including 335 from Cl0p's mass posting.
- **Sources**
  - https://www.ransomware.live/
  - https://api.ransomware.live/v2/victims/2026/10
- **Doubt notes**
  - Gangs inflate their leak sites, re-post old victims and miss victims who paid quietly. Months are grouped by attack/claim date, so recent months can still be revised upward.
  - Cross-check with Chainalysis payment data: https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/

## 5. `FBI IC3 AI-linked scam losses by crime type 2025.png` → **NEW Q4 AI-Enabled Attacks**
- **Title:** FBI: AI-linked scams cost victims $893M in 2025 — $632M in fake investments
- **Caption:** 2025 IC3 complaints referencing AI: $893M losses, top 10 crime types (log scale). Source: FBI IC3 2025.
- **Label:** DATA (self-reported)
- **Bullets**
  - 2025: 22,364 complaints referenced AI, with $893.3M in adjusted losses.
  - Investment scams with an AI link accounted for $632.0M, BEC $30.3M, tech support $19.5M, romance $19.0M and employment $12.6M.
  - IBM 2026: AI-driven attacks rose 56%, and AI-driven malicious breaches averaged $6.04M vs $5.03M for non-AI ones.
- **Sources**
  - https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2025_IC3Report.pdf
  - https://www.ibm.com/reports/data-breach
- **Doubt notes**
  - These are complaints where the victim mentioned AI, so the figures are a floor. The FBI notes overall investment-scam losses exceeded $8B and many victims don't realize AI was involved.
  - The log scale is used so the small categories stay readable; this is stated in the subtitle.
